## 🚀 About Me

I am a **Project & Business Analyst at MB Finance** and a **Computer Engineering student**, working at the intersection of **financial markets, technology, and data**.

My background combines software development with a strong understanding of business needs. I started my career working on **web and mobile applications** and currently contribute to the development and improvement of **internal solutions, automation workflows, data analysis, and technology projects for the financial sector**.

I have hands-on experience with **Python, TypeScript, JavaScript, SQL, React, React Native, Node.js, C#, .NET, and Java**, as well as **API development, data modeling, process automation, Artificial Intelligence, infrastructure, and systems integration**.
